Pork Shumai

  • Shumai Recipe
  • Description: Shumai is a popular Asian steamed dumpling made with ground pork,
    shrimp, and flavorful seasonings wrapped in thin wonton wrappers. It is soft, juicy, and
    delicious, making it a perfect snack, appetizer, or side dish.

  • Ingredients:
    • 500g ground pork
    • 100g shrimp (chopped)
    • 1 small carrot (finely chopped)
    • 1 small onion (minced)
    • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
    • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
    • 1 tablespoon oyster sauce
    • 1 teaspoon sesame oil
    • Salt and pepper
    • Shumai or wonton wrappers
    • Chopped spring onions (optional)

  • Instructions:
    1. Combine the ground pork, chopped shrimp, carrot, onion, and garlic in a bowl.
    2. Add soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, salt, and pepper.
    3. Mix all the ingredients until well combined.
    4. Place a spoonful of filling in the center of each wrapper.
    5. Fold the wrapper around the filling, leaving the top open.
    6. Arrange the shumai in a steamer, leaving space between each piece.
    7. Steam for 15–20 minutes or until the filling is fully cooked.
    8. Remove carefully from the steamer and allow to cool slightly.

  • Serving: Serve warm with soy sauce, calamansi, or chili-garlic sauce.
