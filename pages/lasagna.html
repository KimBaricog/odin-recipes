Lasagna Recipe

  • Lasagna Recipe
  • Description: Lasagna is a delicious Italian dish made with layers of pasta, savory meat sauce, creamy cheese, and tomato sauce. It is baked until the cheese becomes melted and golden, creating a rich and flavorful meal perfect for family gatherings and special occasions.
  • Ingredients:
    • Lasagna noodles
    • Ground beef
    • Tomato sauce
    • Onion (chopped)
    • Garlic (minced)
    • Mozzarella cheese
    • Cheddar cheese
    • Ricotta cheese
    • Salt and pepper
    • Cooking oil
  • Instructions:
    1. Boil the lasagna noodles until tender, then drain.
    2. Heat oil in a pan and sauté the onion and garlic.
    3. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.
    4. Pour in the tomato sauce and season with salt and pepper.
    5. Simmer the sauce for 10–15 minutes.
    6. Spread a layer of meat sauce in a baking dish.
    7. Add lasagna noodles, ricotta cheese, and mozzarella cheese.
    8. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
    9. Top with mozzarella and cheddar cheese.
    10. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 30–40 minutes, until hot and bubbly.
    11. Let it cool for 10 minutes before serving.
  • Serving: Serve warm and enjoy your homemade lasagna with garlic bread or a fresh salad!
