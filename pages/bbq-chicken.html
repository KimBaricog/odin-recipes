BBQ Chicken

  • BBQ Chicken Recipe
  • Description: BBQ Chicken is a delicious and flavorful dish made with tender chicken marinated in a sweet and savory barbecue
    sauce. It is grilled until juicy and perfectly cooked, making it a great meal for family gatherings and special occasions.

  • Ingredients:
    • 1 kg chicken (legs, thighs, or wings)
    • 1 cup barbecue sauce
    • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
    • 2 tablespoons calamansi juice
    • 3 cloves garlic (minced)
    • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
    • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
    • Salt and pepper

  • Instructions:
    1. Prepare the chicken.
    2. Combine barbecue sauce, soy sauce, calamansi juice, garlic, brown sugar, oil, salt, and pepper in a bowl.
    3. Marinate the chicken in the mixture for at least 30 minutes in the refrigerator.
    4. Preheat the grill to medium heat.
    5. Grill the chicken, turning occasionally and brushing with extra BBQ sauce.
    6. Cook until the thickest part reaches 74°C (165°F).
    7. Remove from the grill and let it rest for a few minutes.

  • Serving: Serve hot with rice, vegetables, or your favorite side dish!
